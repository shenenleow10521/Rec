MECHANICAL DEPARTMENT / GROUP 4
Body & chassis

      
A printed body.
A carefully layered build.

    

    

      

        
01 / STRUCTURE
15% INFILL

        
3D printed body

          
    
            
  • 15% infill — high rigidity with minimal weight.
    • 
            
  • Filleted motor towers help prevent snapping under torque.
    • 
            
  • Open-top switch bracket makes switch replacement easy.
    • 
            
  • Integrated fixed jaw is printed with the chassis as one piece.
    • 
          

        

      

      

        
02 / ASSEMBLY
TWO-LAYER FRAME

        
Two-layer frame

          
    
            
  • Elevated donut board — metal standoffs save space and separate underside soldering from the electronics below.
    • 
            
  • Top-mounted Bluetooth module keeps signal transmission unobstructed.
    • 
            
  • Low rear battery lowers the centre of gravity for maximum rear-wheel traction.
    •
